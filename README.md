# GRC Homelab

GRC (governance, risk and compliance) is a broad term referring to internal organizational structure, risk management, and how organizations meet external regulatory requirements.

All businesses are full of risks and so having policy in place to manage risks when they arise and also to anticipate and mitigate risks before they become a problem is vital. 

## Task
This was a group project which we completed in a group of 3. Our task was to choose security area and create a complete all the rish management documentation for it including: Policy, Risk register entry and procedure, and finally a Control-to-Evidence map and one ticket.

We chose "Access control" as our area of interest. The complete assignment and our report can be seen in the pdf's attached.
